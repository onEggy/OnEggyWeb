## **What Are AWS Managed Services?**  

AWS Managed Services (AMS) simplify the complexity of cloud management by automating operations, ensuring compliance, and optimizing infrastructure performance. These services handle tasks like:  
- **Monitoring and Alerting:** Real-time insights into system health.  
- **Backup and Recovery:** Automated backups and disaster recovery solutions.  
- **Scaling and Optimization:** Dynamic scaling to match workload demands.  
- **Security and Compliance:** Adherence to industry standards like GDPR and ISO.
